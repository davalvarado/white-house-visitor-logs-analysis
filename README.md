# White House Visitor Logs Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes White House visitor access records to identify patterns in visit volume, timing, meeting locations, and overall public engagement. The goal was to use data visualization to make visitor log data easier to understand and highlight trends related to transparency and civic access.

## Dataset

- White House WAVES Access Records
- Time Period: January 2023 through May 2023
- Data Files Used:
  - January 2023 Access Records
  - February 2023 Access Records
  - March 2023 Access Records
  - April 2023 Access Records
  - May 2023 Access Records

## Methods

The monthly access record files were combined into a single dataset and prepared for visualization. Date and time fields were converted into usable formats, and new time-based features were created for month, date, day of week, and hour.

Several visualizations were created to analyze visitor activity, including:

1. Monthly Visit Volume
2. Visits by Day of Week
3. Visits by Hour of Day
4. Monthly Visits by Location
5. Top Meeting Areas by Visit Count
6. Cumulative Visits Over Time

## Results

The analysis revealed several patterns in White House visitor access records:

- Visitor volume increased steadily across the first five months of 2023.
- Midweek and morning visits showed strong activity patterns.
- The White House location accounted for the largest share of monthly visit volume.
- Visitor traffic was concentrated in a few major meeting areas, including the East Wing Tour and West Wing.
- More than 216,000 visit records were captured across the five-month period.

## Key Findings

- Public interest and visitor activity remained strong during the period analyzed.
- Visit activity followed clear timing patterns, especially during midweek mornings.
- Space utilization was concentrated in a small number of high-traffic areas.
- Visitor log data can help support transparency and public engagement.
